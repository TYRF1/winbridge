# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Respect the NDA rule: never post vendor names, binaries, screenshots, or
  proprietary logs.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, sexualized content,
doxxing, or publishing others' private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Report incidents to the maintainer privately via GitHub: contact
[@TYRF1](https://github.com/TYRF1). A private security advisory is acceptable
when the report also involves a vulnerability. Maintainers will review and
respond, and may remove, edit, or reject contributions and ban contributors for
behavior they deem inappropriate. They will respect the privacy of the reporter.
